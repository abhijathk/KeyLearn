# Time Keepers' assets are not in this repository

Time Keepers' world, characters, models, textures, horizon art, faces, cards,
sounds and logo are the **AK 3D Pack**: a KeyLearn product sold under a
commercial licence. They are **not** covered by the AGPL that covers
KeyLearn's source code, and they are not published here.

The paths they occupy under `kids-assets/` are listed in `.gitignore`. A
production build merges them in from the private pack with
`scripts/ak-pack-merge.mjs`. A build without them still works; Time Keepers
is then missing its art.

Hero Trail and Dino Run, and their assets in this folder, are part of
KeyLearn and AGPL-licensed with it.

To license the AK 3D Pack, contact KeyLearn support.
